How to play

Move — touch and drag anywhere. A floating stick appears under your thumb; drag to steer. On desktop use the mouse or WASD / arrows.

Attack — automatic. Your weapons fire on their own; you only steer.

Motes — the blue sparks enemies drop are experience. Collect them to level up and choose an upgrade.

Survive — the dark grows stronger every minute, and a Warden arrives now and then. Live as long as you can.

Cinders — every descent earns cinders whether you win or fall. Spend them in The Forge for permanent power, and unlock new Embers.
